# User guide / 使用说明

## 中文

### 1. 导入媒体

在媒体库点“导入文件”选择一个音频或视频，也可以扫描并选择设备媒体。EchoLoop 只保存被选中文件的 URI 引用，不复制或移动原文件。删除媒体库记录不会删除设备上的源文件。

### 2. 播放与循环

- 在媒体库打开一个条目开始播放；进度、字幕和循环设置会按媒体保存。
- 下方进度轨用于跳转；上方选区轨用于拖出 AB 片段，随后可微调 A/B 边界。
- 循环面板可选择无限循环或指定遍数；倍速和跟读停顿也在播放页操作。
- 播放队列可切换顺序播放、单曲循环和列表循环。
- 横屏或平板会根据可用宽度调整布局。播放期间切到后台或锁屏，可从通知栏/锁屏控制播放。

### 3. 导入字幕或生成 AI 句子

在播放页顶部打开字幕菜单，可以导入 SRT、ASS、VTT 字幕、手动添加/编辑字幕，或选择“AI 自动分句”。

首次识别前，打开设置页的“AI 自动分句”区域，填写服务商提供的 HTTPS 地址、模型名和 API Key。默认地址为 `https://api.openai.com/v1`，默认模型名为 `whisper-1`；语言留空时交给服务自动识别，也可以填写 `zh`、`en` 等语言代码。服务商需兼容 OpenAI Whisper transcription endpoint 和 `verbose_json` 时间戳响应。

回到播放页，在字幕菜单中点“AI 自动分句”。识别完成后，字幕区域会切换到按时间排列的精听句子列表。点某句即可从句首开始循环；打开列表顶部的“自动推进”开关后，每句按设置的循环遍数播放，再进入下一句。长按句子可跳转到该句但不循环。

### 4. 数据与隐私

媒体文件保留在原位置；字幕和播放设置保存在应用本地数据库；跟读录音保存在应用私有目录。AI 识别需要联网：用户发起识别后，音频或视频导出的临时音频分段会发送到设置中指定的服务。API Key 通过 Android Keystore 保存。EchoLoop 会在识别请求结束后删除由它生成的临时音频文件。请确认服务商符合你的隐私和数据保留要求。

录音对比需要麦克风权限；后台播放会使用 Android 前台媒体播放服务和通知权限。Android 系统或厂商的电池管理设置可能影响长时间后台播放。

## English

### 1. Import media

Choose **Import file** in the library, or scan and select media already on the device. EchoLoop stores a URI reference; it does not copy or move the original file. Removing a library entry does not delete the source media.

### 2. Playback and loops

- Open a library item to play it. Progress, subtitles, and loop settings are saved per media item.
- Use the lower timeline to seek and the upper selection timeline to mark an AB range; fine-tune its A and B points in the loop controls.
- Choose infinite or counted repeats in the loop panel. Playback speed and speaking gaps are available on the player page.
- The playback queue supports sequential, repeat-one, and repeat-all modes.
- Landscape and tablet layouts adapt to the available width. While playing, use the notification or lock-screen controls after switching apps or locking the screen.

### 3. Import subtitles or generate AI sentence cues

Open the subtitle menu at the top of the player to import SRT, ASS, or VTT subtitles, add or edit subtitles, or choose **AI sentence segmentation**.

Before the first transcription, open **Settings → AI sentence segmentation** and enter the provider's HTTPS endpoint, model name, and API key. The defaults are `https://api.openai.com/v1` and `whisper-1`. Leave language blank for provider detection, or enter a code such as `zh` or `en`. The service must support the OpenAI Whisper transcription endpoint and `verbose_json` timestamp responses.

Return to the player and choose **AI sentence segmentation** from the subtitle menu. After transcription, the subtitle area shows sentence cues in time order. Tap a sentence to loop it from its start. Turn on **Auto advance** at the top of the list to repeat each sentence the configured number of times and then move to the next one. Long-press a sentence to seek to it without starting a loop.

### 4. Data and privacy

Source media stays where it is. Subtitles and playback settings are kept in the app's local database; practice recordings stay in the app's private storage. AI transcription requires a network connection. When you start it, EchoLoop sends the audio or temporary audio chunks extracted from video to the configured provider. The API key is stored through Android Keystore. EchoLoop removes temporary audio files it created after the transcription request finishes. Check that the provider's privacy and retention terms meet your needs.

Recording comparison needs microphone permission. Background playback uses Android's foreground media playback service and notification permission. Android or device-maker battery management may affect long-running background playback.
